How we research

Manufacturer specifications

Every specification comes from the manufacturer’s published product information. When a spec isn’t published, we say so instead of guessing.

Analysis, not ride testing

We haven’t ridden or tested these bikes. Range figures are labeled as manufacturer claims, and genuine price cuts are distinguished from bundle value and retailer-claimed discounts.
