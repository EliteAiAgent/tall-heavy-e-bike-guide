Best Step-Through E-Bikes for Heavy Riders

Published: October 2026 · Prices verified: October 6, 2026 (re-check before buying)

Why step-through matters at weight

Step-through frames eliminate standover height concerns entirely — meaningful if mounting a tall step-over frame is difficult, or if joint or mobility issues come with the territory. The trade-off: among genuinely high-payload bikes, step-through options are fewer than step-overs. Every bike below is a step-through (or offers a step-through frame) with a manufacturer-published payload, drawn from the same spec research as our main comparison.

We have not ridden or tested these bikes. All specs come from the manufacturers' published specifications, linked per bike.

The bikes, in brief

Mokwheel Basalt ST 2.0 — the only 450-lb step-through we've found

The standout of this list: a genuine step-through frame rated to 450 lbs, with the biggest battery here (940Wh, claimed 80-mile range) and sizing from 5′2″ to 6′8″. Currently $1,799.99 after a verified $200 cut.
Limitations: 80 lbs; treat the $1,999.99 reference price with appropriate caution. Source: mokwheel.com

Check the Basalt ST 2.0 price

Velotric Discover 2 — the deal in step-through form

440-lb payload, 63 lbs (the lightest full-size option in our research), comfortable commuter geometry, and the deepest verified true cut we've found: $500 off, now $1,499. The 75Nm motor is the mildest here — fine on flats, less eager on steep hills at full load.
Limitations: lowest torque in this group; claimed range assumes a 187-lb rider. Source: velotricbike.com

Check the Discover 2 price

Aventon Aventure 3 Step-Through — the warranty pick

400-lb payload, fat 26×4.0″ tires, 733Wh LG-cell battery. Aventon's frame warranty — 2 years, extended to lifetime if you register within 90 days — is the best long-term frame deal in our research if you remember to register. $1,999, no current discount.
Limitations: no discount; Aventon doesn't publish standover height; 76–77 lbs. Source: aventon.com

Ride1Up Vorsa ST — the torque pick

95Nm is the highest hub-motor torque in our research, on a 65-lb chassis with 440-lb payload and full published geometry. From $1,495.
Limitations: battery figure is our computation, not manufacturer-stated; 1-year warranty is the thinnest here; no discount. Source: ride1up.com

Lectric XPeak 2.0 Step-Through — the budget option, with a warning

85Nm motor, fat tires, both frame styles, currently $1,299 after a verified $200 cut. But the 330-lb payload cap leaves zero cargo margin at 330 lbs — this is only for riders who travel light. Heavier riders should step up to the 400+ lb bikes above.
Limitations: see the margin warning; that's the whole story.

Our analysis: how to choose

Our analysis based on the manufacturer specifications above.

  • Heaviest riders (380+ lbs): the Basalt ST 2.0 is the only step-through with real headroom. Everything else here tops out at 440.
  • Best value: Discover 2 at $1,499 — the $500 verified cut makes it the cheapest 440-lb step-through by a wide margin.
  • Hilly routes: Vorsa ST's 95Nm hub motor is the strongest climber in step-through form here.
  • Long-term ownership: Aventure 3 ST's register-for-lifetime frame warranty is unmatched if you'll keep the bike for years.
